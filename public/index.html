OBS setup

Use a camera’s OBS URL to pair a phone directly. Keep Camera Studio open for Program switching and for USB or MJPEG sources.

  1. In OBS, add a Browser source under Sources.
  2. Paste the URL. Match the Browser Source and OBS canvas sizes. Use 1920 × 1080 for landscape or 1080 × 1920 for portrait.
  3. Scan the QR on the OBS preview to pair. The pairing screen clears as soon as video arrives.
  4. Leave Shutdown source when not visible and Refresh browser when scene becomes active off.
  5. For phone audio, enable the phone’s microphone and turn on Control audio via OBS in the Browser Source.
Portrait and landscape video

On the phone, leave Auto-rotate on. Unlock screen rotation, or allow motion access if your phone stays locked. Use Fit entire frame in OBS framing to keep the whole picture when it turns. The same URL works with either OBS canvas size.

Install on a phone

Open your HTTPS AirCast address. On iPhone use Share and Add to Home Screen. On Android use Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu.

Network and browser access

Open the same HTTPS-hosted page on all devices for phone pairing. A local file works for testing on this computer. Wireless pairing uses the PeerJS signaling service and works best on the same Wi‑Fi. Networks that block peer-to-peer traffic can require a TURN relay. The phone releases its camera and slot when you leave its view.
